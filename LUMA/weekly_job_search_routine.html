Weekly job search routine by day

Monday ~2.5 hrs total Reset + launch day
8:00 am — 20 min
Reset your tracker Monday only
Open your job search spreadsheet. Clear "pending" statuses from last week. Mark anything that got no response in 7+ days as closed. You're starting a clean week.
8:20 am — 20 min
Source and save roles for the week Monday only
Check Ashby, LinkedIn saved searches, and your 7 target company career pages. Save 10–15 roles to your tracker. You are not applying yet — you are building this week's pipeline.
8:40 am — 25 min
LinkedIn outreach batch high ROI
Send 3–5 personalized DMs to current SDRs or AEs at target companies. One sentence on why you're reaching out, one genuine question. No referral ask yet — build rapport first.
9:05 am — 75 min
Apply — 3 to 5 roles high ROI
Pull from your tracker (roles you saved today or last Friday). Customize your cover letter for each — should take ~10 min per application using your template. Never send a generic copy-paste.
Afternoon — 10 min
Respond to any replies high ROI
Reply to any DMs or emails same day. Speed signals exactly what SDR managers are hiring for. Never let a reply sit 24+ hours.
Afternoon — 5 min
Log activity in tracker
Log all applications sent and DMs sent today. Totals only — keep it fast.
Tuesday ~2 hrs total Core cadence
8:00 am — 25 min
LinkedIn outreach batch high ROI
Send 3–5 new personalized DMs to SDRs or AEs at target companies. Don't repeat Monday's targets — move to new people at the same or different companies.
8:25 am — 75 min
Apply — 3 to 5 roles high ROI
Pull from your tracker. Customize cover letter per application. Tuesday applications get among the fastest recruiter response rates of the week — use this day well.
9:40 am — 10 min
Follow-up check medium ROI
Review DMs sent last Tuesday (7 days ago). Anyone who didn't reply gets one polite follow-up — then they're closed. One follow-up max per person, ever.
Afternoon — 10 min
Respond to any replies high ROI
Check DMs and email. Respond same day to anything that came in.
Afternoon — 5 min
Log activity in tracker
Update applications sent, DMs sent, and any replies received.
Wednesday ~2 hrs total Core cadence
8:00 am — 25 min
LinkedIn outreach batch high ROI
Send 3–5 new DMs. By Wednesday you should have momentum — if anyone replied Mon or Tue, think about who else at that company is worth reaching out to.
8:25 am — 75 min
Apply — 3 to 5 roles high ROI
Pull from your tracker. Wednesday is your last high-value application day — Thurs/Fri submissions get reviewed later. Prioritize your strongest applications today.
9:40 am — 10 min
Follow-up check medium ROI
Review DMs sent last Wednesday (7 days ago). Send one follow-up to anyone who hasn't replied. Move on after that.
Afternoon — 10 min
Respond to any replies high ROI
Same-day response to anything in DMs or email.
Afternoon — 5 min
Log activity in tracker
Update your tracker with today's applications and outreach.
Thursday ~1.5 hrs total Core cadence
8:00 am — 25 min
LinkedIn outreach batch high ROI
Send 3–5 new DMs. Keep the cadence even if energy dips mid-week — outreach sent Thursday gets responses Monday morning, keeping next week's pipeline warm.
8:25 am — 60 min
Apply — 3 to 5 roles high ROI
Fewer applications today is fine — 3 quality applications beats 5 rushed ones. Pull from whatever remains in your tracker from Monday's sourcing session.
9:25 am — 10 min
Follow-up check medium ROI
Review DMs sent last Thursday. One follow-up to non-responders, then close them out.
Afternoon — 10 min
Respond to any replies high ROI
Same-day responses to all DMs and emails.
Afternoon — 5 min
Log activity in tracker
Keep your tracker current.
Friday ~1.5 hrs total Review + close day
8:00 am — 10 min
Respond to any replies high ROI
Clear your inbox first. Any reply left over the weekend loses momentum. Respond before anything else.
8:10 am — 10 min
Follow-up check medium ROI
Review DMs sent last Friday. Send one follow-up to non-responders. This is your last outreach of the week — keep it warm but not needy.
8:20 am — 30 min
Weekly review Friday only
Count totals: applications sent, DMs sent, replies received, conversations active. Ask yourself: what got a response this week and why? What got zero traction? Adjust one thing for next week — not everything.
8:50 am — 30 min
Source roles for next week Friday only
Check Ashby, LinkedIn saved searches, and your 7 target company career pages. Save 10–15 roles to your tracker. You will not apply today — this is next Monday's pipeline. Do not apply to roles on Fridays.
Rest of day
No applications today Friday rule
Applications submitted Friday sit at the bottom of a pile that fills up by Tuesday morning. Save your energy. You've done the work — let the week close.
Saturday – Sunday 0 hrs Full off
All day
No applications, no DMs, no sourcing
Weekends are off. Companies do not review applications on weekends. Recruiters are not active. Your rest directly protects the quality of your Monday through Wednesday — the days that actually matter. Burnout is the #1 job search killer. The searches that fail are the ones that spike and crash, not the ones that go slow and steady.
